Four papers, one shared subject: the orchestration shell that sits between a language model and the work it's supposed to do. Today's digest covers caching, compositional workflow synthesis, governance-by-design, and corpus-level trace diagnostics — all attacking different layers of the same agentic stack. Together they sketch a cleaner, faster, safer version of what production agent platforms need to be.

The fit with PYRANA is unusually direct. The caching paper maps onto PlannerDelegatorWorkflow latency and CxU retrieval freshness. The AgentCo-op synthesis work challenges our hand-authored YAML config approach. The governance paper is the clearest published articulation of what pyrana-audit and the output gateway are trying to be. And the trace diagnostics paper describes what pyrana-telemetry should grow into. All four carry concrete Monday-morning implications.

Papers covered:

  • Evaluating Temporal Semantic Caching and Workflow Optimization in Agentic Plan-Execute Pipelines — Classifying queries by time-sensitivity before caching cuts median pipeline latency from 34 seconds to under 10.
  • \methodname: Retrieval-Based Synthesis of Interoperable Multi-Agent Workflows — Automatically composing workflow graphs by retrieval over a skill library beats hand-authored configs on most benchmarks.
  • Governance by Construction for Generalist Agents — Embeds policy constraints into agent architecture at build time rather than patching them on at runtime.
  • Insights Generator: Systematic Corpus-Level Trace Diagnostics for LLM Agents — Analyzes agent trace corpora to surface systematic failure patterns rather than debugging individual runs.

Takeaways for our platform:

  • Ship the two risk-free MCP wins in GenericExecutor this sprint — disk-cached tool discovery and DAG-parallel execution of independent plan steps — and defer temporal semantic caching until we measure actual query paraphrase repetition on JNJ and recurring workflows, since a wrong cache hit returning a stale CxU is worse than a clean miss in any compliance context.
  • Audit our YAML-driven workflow authoring against the AgentCo-op retrieval-synthesis approach — if our skill and tool library is large enough, auto-composing graphs from task descriptions could replace hand-wired configs and cut onboarding time for new tenants.
  • Map the governance-by-construction constraints from paper three directly onto the pyrana-audit and output gateway spec, and scope pyrana-telemetry's next iteration around corpus-level pattern detection from paper four rather than single-trace inspection alone.
